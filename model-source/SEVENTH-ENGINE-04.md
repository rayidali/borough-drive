## Changes

- **Camera:** right-drag orbits/looks; Shift + right-drag or middle-drag pans up toward roofs; wheel zooms; V recenters only the camera. All four selected views support inspection. C selects POV and R resets the car. Mouse inspection leaves the vehicle transform unchanged, ignores menus/pause and releases cleanly over the HUD.
- **Addresses:** the label beside the speedometer uses the block's 39 actual frontage intervals, including 48½, combined 73–75 and the avenue-addressed corner faces. It changes with the car's position/heading, holds boundaries briefly to avoid flicker and clears outside this block. It is a nearest-frontage indication, not surveyed GPS.
- **Architecture:** corrected ground openings, raised and at-grade entrances, basement access, masonry and storefront partitions. Examples include Kinka on the east/right at 63, Abraço below the right parlor at 81, the red at-grade door at 53, oak/fanlight entry at 55, blue ground at 58, gray stone at 62, scaffold at 66, 79's separate residential/shop entrance, white tile and painted upper brick at 82, and 118 Second's gray ground and prominent fire escapes. Big Bar now has a black awning, stacked white name and blue-over-yellow lower panels.
- **Street:** source-positioned trees and fixtures, an open-wheel parked bicycle, two observed dining enclosures, basement pavement openings with protective collision, and dated fire-site fencing, bracing, salvaged stone/ironwork, barriers and the remaining wall. Proposed LPC construction is excluded. No Citi Bike dock was found on this segment in the inspected dated views and bounded station feed.
- **Rendering:** equivalent static materials share batches; redundant weather-uniform updates are bounded. A 48 m street-bucket experiment increased draw calls and was reverted. The final game keeps all modeled geometry and individual building culling. Existing car physics, drifting and authored sound are unchanged.
