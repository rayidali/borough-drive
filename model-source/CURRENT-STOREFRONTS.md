## 160 First Avenue — Nishaan

- Best full front: `nishaan-welcome.jpg` (768×768), official https://eatnishaan.com . Exact image URL in `official-supplement-sources.json`.
- Best awning/blade detail: `nishaan-storefront.jpg` (1920×1200), official https://eatnishaan.com . Exact image URL in `official-image-sources.json`.
- Royal/cobalt blue pitched awning, matching blue window/door frames. Coral/pink-red bold italic uppercase NISHAAN in an irregular cream cartouche, small cream Urdu lettering beneath. Cream tagline and 160 1ST AVE on blue valance, thin double cream border. Cream HALAL emblem on triangular awning side.
- Full front has single broad glazed door at viewer-left, brass vertical pull on its right edge; larger right section divided into three narrow vertical glazed panels, with blue mullions. Door occupies roughly one third of the glass frontage, right group two thirds. Horizontal transom across front at the tops of the lower panels; upper transom left/right divided by central blue post. Rightmost panel partly obscured in photo.
- Upper pitched sign face about 3.4:1 width:height in frontal photo; narrow valance roughly 10:1. The awning projects beyond both jambs.
- Hanging blade is a blue horizontal rectangle in a dark metal frame, about 2:1 W:H, with cream/white scalloped postage-stamp panel. Red SANDWICHES, smaller red CHOPPED-MELTED-LOADED, black outlined down-left arrow, row of small ochre floral marks.
- Visible furnishings: honey-colored timber tables/chairs or stools near front, some stacked/inverted. Cardboard box inside lower left. Open cellar hatch and two orange cones outside are temporary conditions, not permanent restaurant furniture.
- Capture dates unknown. `nishaan-ev-large.jpg` is an earlier preopening photo published June 30, 2025 with Sabor A Mexico sign and COMING SOON banner; do not use it as current signage. Eater July 9, 2025 describes an inherited interior arch and planned 10–14 seats, but those are textual design descriptions, not visible evidence in the current façade photos.

- Best façade: `goodtime-ev-large.jpg` (5712×4284), https://evgrieve.com/2026/06/retro-signage-alert-good-time-country.html . Exact image URL in `large-image-sources.json`.

- Broad white/ivory rectangular sign with thin silver border, roughly 8:1 W:H. Red square clock occupies left ~13%; white 12/3/6/9 and slash ticks, black hands. Black/dark brown GOOD above TIME just right of clock (~14% of sign width); very large dark brown italic Country Buffet occupies remainder, pale gold/cream edging and slight depth/shadow.
- Warm cream/off-white rough-stucco façade. Wide closed silver perforated roll-down grille at left; narrow recessed cream door at right. Main grille occupies roughly 63% of overall cream frontage; grille is roughly 3.5× as wide as door leaf. Right door bay includes substantial cream piers and head panel. This refines earlier quick estimate: 72% was too large for the grille relative to full façade.
- Door: white/cream frame, brass vertical pull at left, large lower solid panel. Upper glazing has 3×3 decorative arrangement: three tiny top rectangles/squares, tall narrow sides around a broad tall central pane, three tiny bottom rectangles/squares. Blue painter's tape around glazing and brown paper/notices are temporary preparation coverings.
- No finished interior furnishings observable through shutter. Two green-capped fire-department connections at lower left; small utility fittings/electrical box on pier between grille and door.
- Upper story immediately above has red brick, broad dark windows and two painted colorful mask-like panels. These are visible above this same 166 sign, not evidence of address 164.
